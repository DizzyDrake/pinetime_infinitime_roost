# Development history

Aurora began Roost as a haptic breathing addition to InfiniTime 1.16.0. The design grew from preset patterns to a four-phase custom editor, persistent settings, screen-independent timing, and call/alarm priority. GPT-6 and collaborating agents assisted with C++ integration, testing, and technical documentation. Aurora handled the physical watch trials and selected the launcher artwork.

The RC2 source was assembled from four original integration patches, two later timing-guard fixes, and a comment-only correction. The exact seven patches are preserved in `patches/`; they are not needed for everyday installation or source integration.

The released Roost variant is deliberately distinct from Aurora's personal firmware customizations.
